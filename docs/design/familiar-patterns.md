# Familiar patterns: how the app is laid out, and where that comes from

Written 2026-09-09, after the map redesign. The brief was that the app
looked complicated: too many tabs, too many nouns (stand, floor, hall,
office, room, coach, mine), and a home screen that tried to be a chat, a
to-do list and a dashboard at once. People will not learn a new building
to use an app; they will use the shape they already know from the apps
on their phone.

## What the popular apps in this space do

Sources are listed at the end. Several pages were behind the sandbox's
network policy, so the detail below is what the search summaries carried
plus what is common knowledge about these apps; nothing here is quoted
from a page that could not be read.

- **Headspace** stripped its navigation down to a *Today* tab after user
  research, with one-tap access to a few activities for the time of day,
  and pushed the whole catalogue behind *Explore*. The critique of it is
  useful too: what people liked about the old layout was "one step at a
  time" and a decluttered space, and the redesign is faulted for slow
  loading, not for being simple.
- **Finch** opens on the pet and a short daily checklist; you set your
  own goals, and each one ticked fills a progress bar. Journey and
  history live elsewhere. The praise is for immediate, visible feedback
  on completing a goal.
- **Noom** replaced its old wheel with a task list that shows the day's
  progress and goals at a glance, introduces the coach a few days in, and
  uses progressive disclosure: only what is needed now, features revealed
  as days pass.
- **Duolingo** refreshed its core tabs in 2026 to be "cleaner, clearer,
  and more cohesive", after criticism that several tab icons were
  unrecognisable. Its home has always been one thing: the path, with the
  next lesson lit.
- **Onboarding guides** (Appcues, UXCam, Eleken, VWO) agree on the same
  points: find the one action that predicts retention and design the
  flow to reach it first; short, personalised, punctuated by a first
  action; value inside sixty seconds.

## What the app now does

| Pattern | Where it came from | In FounderFloor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A *Today* tab that shows one thing to do and a short list | Headspace, Noom, Finch | Today: the next task with a Start button, this week's three tasks as a checklist, two small doors (log the week, read it back) |
| Three or four tabs, plain words, recognisable icons | Duolingo's refresh, tab-bar guidance | Today · Map · Coach · You |
| The journey as a path with the next stop lit | Duolingo, Finch's journey | The map: the building floor by floor, the keeper in the room of the week |
| A chat with a picker at the top for who answers | Every assistant app | Coach: the desk, or one of four coaches, chosen from the pill at the top |
| A profile tab that is a list of doors, not work | Every app's *You* / *Profile* | You: your company, the Office, plan, notebook, drafts, inbox, coaches, floor, plans, settings |
| Progressive disclosure: the social part later | Noom | The floor (other founders' stands) is behind You, not a tab |
| Immediate feedback on completing a goal | Finch | Ticks transition, a task finishing bursts, the week gets a score |

What was cut from the front: the hall picker (it now lives only on the
floor page, where it changes something), the "up next" card on the chat
(it is Today now), the stand, office and floor as tabs.
