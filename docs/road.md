# The road

Seven stops from a start-up dream to a start-up, in words a child follows. Today shows the whole road with where you are and what to do now; You shows it as a strip; every page that belongs to a stop says so at the top. Defined in `packages/shared/src/road.ts`, read from the building in `apps/mobile/src/lib/road.ts`, drawn by `apps/mobile/src/components/Road.tsx`.

| Stop | Title | What to do | Done when | Where |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Say your idea | Write one sentence: what you make, and who it is for. | The sign on the stand is written | Your company |
| 2 | Make your plan | Answer eight questions. The desk writes your first four weeks. | A plan exists | The eight questions |
| 3 | Ask five people | Find five people who might pay. Ask them. Write down what they say. | Five interviews in the Office | The Office |
| 4 | Do this week | Open a task, follow the steps, tick it. Three a week. | Three tasks ticked | Today, the plan, a task |
| 5 | See your app | The studio draws your app from your words. Show it to the five people. | The Workshop has drawn the founder's own product | The Workshop |
| 6 | Build it | Send the brief to Lovable or Claude Code. | The brief was shared at least once | The Workshop |
| 7 | First paying customer | Someone pays real money. Log it on Friday. | A logged week with a customer, or money on the stand | The Office |

The stops are in order but not a cage: a founder who skips ahead sees the stop behind them still open, and it is the one to do now. A stop that is partly done says how far ("3 of 5").
